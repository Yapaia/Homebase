## Radio that travels with you

Streaming eats your data and drops out in dead zones. Real radio does not. Yapaia Beat receives FM and DAB+ with an inexpensive **RTL-SDR USB stick** – right on your Home Assistant computer, without any internet.

## What Beat can do

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **FM and DAB+** | FM with RDS (station name, radio text, traffic programme flag) and DAB+ with track info and slideshow. |
| **Scan and favourites** | Let it scan once, tap your favourite stations, find them by name or frequency. |
| **Station logos** | Fetched automatically and cached, upload your own – or have a nice one generated. |
| **Station following** | Leaving the coverage area? Beat switches to the next frequency, or from FM to DAB+ and back. |
| **Plays where you want** | Through Home Assistant’s speakers or as an MP3 stream to any device. |
| **Two radio cards** | For your dashboard: retro with wood or modern and dark. |

![The Yapaia Beat web interface](/assets/bilder/beat-webui.png)

## Together with Go

If Yapaia Go is installed too, a navigation announcement can blend into the running programme: music goes down, the announcement plays, music comes back. Without Go, Beat works perfectly on its own.
