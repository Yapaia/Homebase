## Hands free, mind free

While driving, you do not tap around on screens. With Yapaia Satellite you simply say “Take me to Magdeburg” – and Yapaia Go calculates the route. Or ask about the weather, switch the lights, or get your arrival time read out.

## What’s inside

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Hardware** | AI-Thinker ESP32 Audio Kit V2.2 (A436) with two microphones, amplifier and six buttons – for a few euros. |
| **Voice in** | The microphones listen; recognition is done by Home Assistant’s voice assistant. |
| **Voice out** | Answers, announcements and music through the attached speaker. |
| **Wake word** | In Home Assistant – including your own, like “Yapaia” – or directly on the device. |
| **Buttons and light** | Talk, mute, louder, quieter, stop. The LED shows when it is listening. |

## Several satellites

One in the cab, one in the over-cab bed, one outside under the awning: every satellite is a device of its own. The Yapaia integration finds them all and tells the other modules – so Go can make an announcement on all of them or just on one.
