## In short

This website collects no data about you. There are **no tracking cookies, no analytics, no ads and no embedded third-party services**. Fonts and images are loaded from this server, not from Google or anyone else.

## What the server still sees

When you open a page, the web server processes technically necessary data: your IP address, date and time, the requested address and your browser’s identifier. This data is used only to deliver the page and is not stored permanently (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest in secure operation).

## The one cookie

If you pick a different design in the preview, the site remembers it in a cookie called `design`. It contains only the design’s name and is not analysed.

## Yapaia itself

The Yapaia modules run in your vehicle. They send no data to us or to third parties. Only if you enter a map server does Yapaia Go download map packages from it.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and objection, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact: see [legal notice](/en/impressum/).
